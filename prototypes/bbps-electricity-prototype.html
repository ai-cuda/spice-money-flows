BBPS · Electricity bill

Clickable prototype of an adhikari paying a customer's electricity bill, rebuilt from the Spice Money app (v6.6.62) and the screens seen live on staging. The screens use the app's own text, and every call goes to an in-page mock server with the real BBPS endpoint paths and field names. The electricity boards are the real state distribution companies, but the consumers, bills and amounts are made up: real bill data comes from the board through Bharat BillPay.
