AePS · NSDL eKYC onboarding (KO and Sub-KO)

Clickable prototype of an adhikari completing NSDL Payments Bank onboarding before doing AePS, then adding a staff member (Sub-Adhikari), rebuilt from the Spice Money app (v6.6.62) with the app's own screens, copy, logos and illustrations. Every call goes to an in-page mock server using the real NSDL eKYC endpoint paths and field names. The adhikari, staff, address and phone numbers are made up; consent, terms, benefits and help texts are server-driven in the real app and are representative here.

What the adhikari needs

Adhikari (KO)

📱Registered mobile numberAlready on file; verified by 6-digit OTP
🪪PANAlready on file; one tap to verify against name and DOB. No PAN card upload
🏪Shop addressAlready on file; one tap to confirm
🔢Aadhaar numberTyped (12 digits) with Aadhaar consent
☝️Live fingerprintL1 biometric device (Mantra, Morpho, Startek, Marc11, Evolute, VisionTek, Precision) with its RD Service app

Staff (Sub-KO), optional

👤Name, mobile, date of birth, PANTyped; email optional; age 18–80; staff mobile OTP
☝️Staff Aadhaar + fingerprintThen the adhikari's own Aadhaar + fingerprint to approve

Not asked for anywhere in the app

✕No document upload or photo: no PAN card image, selfie, shop photo, address proof, cheque, GST or certificates. No fee.
